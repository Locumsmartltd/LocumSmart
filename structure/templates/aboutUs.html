Our story

LocumSmart wasn't built around technology. It was built by listening to healthcare providers.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, pharmacies needed more than extra staff — they needed reliable healthcare professionals who could adapt quickly to changing services while maintaining exceptional patient care.

Our journey began by supporting pharmacies with trained vaccination staff, before expanding into pharmacist, pharmacy technician and dispenser recruitment across the UK. As community pharmacy services evolved — from vaccination programmes to Pharmacy First, permanent pharmacy recruitment, GP surgeries and wider healthcare services — our focus never changed: understand exactly what every client needs, vet every candidate carefully, and deliver a service that leaves no loose ends.

Today, our recruitment, compliance, operations and administrative teams work together so every healthcare provider receives candidates who are not only qualified, but genuinely suited to the role and the organisation.

— The LocumSmart team

Supporting pharmacies where recruitment was hardest.

Our reputation grew across Devon, Cornwall, Scotland and other hard-to-recruit regions, where healthcare providers needed dependable staffing support they couldn't get elsewhere.

2023

A growing candidate network meant faster response times, better workforce planning, and more staffing flexibility for clients across the UK.
